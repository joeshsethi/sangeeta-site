# How to edit your website

You can add stories, change your bio, and swap photos yourself — no coding.
Changes go live about a minute after you save.

Your site: https://www.sangeeta-s-kurtz.com
Your editor: https://app.pagescms.org

## One-time setup (about 5 minutes)

1. **Create a free GitHub account** at https://github.com/signup — this is only a login.
   Use any email and pick any username.
2. **Accept the invitation.** Joesh will send an invite; GitHub emails you a link.
   Open it and click **Accept invitation**.
3. **Open the editor** at https://app.pagescms.org → **Sign in with GitHub** →
   allow access → choose the **sangeeta-site** project.

Bookmark https://app.pagescms.org. That's the only page you'll ever need.

## What you can change

Down the left side you'll see:

| Screen | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Home page** | Your main photo, the photo credit, your bio, and the short "Work" list |
| **Work page** | All your articles, grouped into sections, plus the magazine clippings |
| **Talks & Media** | The talks and appearances list on the home page |
| **Projects, Podcasts, & Newsletters** | The projects list on the home page |
| **Settings** | Your name, Instagram, LinkedIn, contact email |

## Common tasks

### Add a new article
1. Click **Work page**.
2. Find the right section (*Features & Investigations* or *Profiles*) and click
   **Add** under **Articles**.
3. Fill in **Title**, **One-line description**, and **Link** (paste the full web address).
4. Click **Save** (top right).

New items appear at the bottom. Drag the handle on the left to reorder.
To delete one, use the **⋯** menu on that item.

### Add something to the home page's short "Work" list
Click **Home page** → **Add** under the "Work" list → Title, Link, Publication → **Save**.
This list is separate from the Work page on purpose, so you choose what gets featured.

### Change your bio
Click **Home page**, edit the **Bio** boxes (one box per paragraph), then **Save**.

### Change a photo
Go to the screen that has it → click the photo field → **Upload** → pick the file → **Save**.
Landscape photos around 2000 pixels wide or more look best.

### Add a talk or a project
Click **Talks & Media** or **Projects, Podcasts, & Newsletters** → **Add** →
fill in the fields → **Save**.

## Two small formatting tricks

These work in the bio and in article descriptions:

- **A link:** `[the words people click](https://the-address.com)`
- **Italics:** `*put asterisks around the words*`

## Good to know

- **You can't break the site.** Every save is kept as a version, so anything can be
  undone. If something looks wrong, tell Joesh.
- **Changes take about a minute.** Refresh the site; if you still see the old version,
  wait a moment and refresh again.
- The editor won't let you save a link that isn't a real web address.
- The design — fonts, colours, layout — is fixed on purpose. You're editing the words
  and pictures, not the layout.

Questions → Joesh.
