# How to edit your website

You can add stories, change your bio, and swap photos yourself — no coding. Changes go live about a minute after you save.

## One-time setup (5 minutes)

1. **Create a free GitHub account** at https://github.com/signup — this is just your login. Use any email and pick a username.
2. **Accept the invitation** — Joesh will send an email from GitHub inviting you to the website's project. Click "Accept invitation."
3. **Open the editor** at https://app.pagescms.org → click **Sign in with GitHub** → choose the `sangeeta-site` project.

That's it. Bookmark https://app.pagescms.org — that's the only place you'll ever need to go.

## The editor

Down the left side you'll see the sections of the site:

| Section | What you can change |
|---|---|
| **About (Home page)** | Bio text, your portrait, photo credit, the small labels under your name, how many pieces show on the home page |
| **Work** | Your articles, grouped in sections (Features & Investigations, Profiles). The magazine clippings strip. |
| **Speaking** | Talks, podcasts, and TV/radio appearances, plus the header photo |
| **Projects** | Podcasts, newsletters, and other projects, plus the header photo |
| **Contact** | The intro line on the contact page |
| **Settings** | Your name, tagline, Instagram/LinkedIn links, contact email |

## Common tasks

### Add a new article
1. Click **Work**.
2. Find the section it belongs in (e.g. *Features & Investigations*) and click **+ Add** under *Articles*.
3. Fill in **Title**, **One-line blurb**, and **Link** (paste the full URL). *Publication* is optional — it's detected from the link automatically (e.g. The Cut).
4. Click **Save** (top right).

New items appear at the bottom of the list. To move one, drag it using the handle on the left. To remove one, click the **⋯** / trash icon on it.

*Tip: to italicize a word in a blurb — a magazine title, say — wrap it in asterisks: `*Greenlights*`.*

### Change your bio
1. Click **About (Home page)**.
2. Edit **Bio — main sentence** and/or **Bio — second line**.
3. Click **Save**.

To link some text, write it like this: `[New York Magazine](https://nymag.com/)`.

### Change a photo
1. Go to the page (About, Speaking, or Projects).
2. Click the photo field → **Upload** → choose the image from your computer.
3. Click **Save**.

Photos look best as a landscape JPG around 1500–2500px wide. The site shows them in black and white automatically.

### Add a talk or project
Same as adding an article — click **Speaking** or **Projects** → **+ Add** → fill in the fields → **Save**.

## Good to know

- **You can't break the site.** Every save is kept as a version. If something looks wrong, tell Joesh and it can be rolled back instantly.
- **Changes take about a minute** to appear. Refresh the site; if you still see the old version, wait a moment and refresh again.
- Required fields are marked; the editor won't let you save a link that isn't a real URL.
- The design itself (fonts, layout, colors) is fixed on purpose — you're editing the words and pictures, not the layout.
