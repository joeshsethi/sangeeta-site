About

Sangeeta Singh-Kurtz is a freelance journalist who investigates intriguing people. She was a staff writer at New York Magazine for five years. She lives in Tokyo, where she recently completed her debut novel.

She hosted Land of the Giants: Dating Games, the award-winning narrative podcast from Vox and The Verge, and has appeared on CNN, NPR, Marketplace, and Offline with Jon Favreau.
